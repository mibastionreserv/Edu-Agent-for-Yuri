## Empiricism ties it together
Every Scrum element serves empiricism: artifacts create transparency; events provide regular inspection; the outcomes of events drive adaptation.

## The mapping
Sprint Planning adapts the plan; the Daily Scrum inspects daily progress and adapts the day; the Review inspects the Increment and adapts the Product Backlog; the Retrospective inspects the process and adapts how the team works.

## Common anti-patterns
Avoid these: extending a Sprint; running the Daily as a manager status report; treating the Scrum Master as a project manager or secretary; a Product Owner 'by committee'; having no Definition of Done; using velocity as a target or to compare teams; skipping the Retrospective; and ending a Sprint with no usable Increment.

## Values in action
Living the values — commitment, focus, openness, respect, courage — is what makes the mechanics work; without them Scrum becomes empty ceremony.
